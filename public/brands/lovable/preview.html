Guidelines

Do's & Don'ts

Do 鈥?Embrace
  • Pure White (#ffffff) as primary canvas
  • Soft Blush and Whisper for surface variation
  • Deep Charcoal (#1a1614) for all primary text
  • Lovable Rose (#e2557a) for primary CTAs only
  • 10px radius for buttons, 16px for cards
  • 4px-based spacing with generous gaps (48px+)
  • Warm shadows with low opacity
  • Sage Green for success states
Don't 鈥?Avoid
  • Pure black (#000000) for body text
  • Lovable Rose for non-interactive decoration
  • Hard, sharp corners (0px radius)
  • Cool/blue-tinted grays for neutrals
  • Heavy, dark drop shadows
  • Dense packing without breathing room
  • Mono fonts for non-code content
  • More than two accent colors per view
