Guidelines

Do's & Don'ts

Do — Embrace
  • Nero Assoluto (#000000) as primary canvas
  • Rosso Corsa exclusively for CTAs and accents
  • Uppercase Futura with 0.08em+ letter-spacing
  • Carbon fiber texture for premium surfaces
  • Sharp, minimal radii (2-4px maximum)
  • 32px+ section gaps for commanding presence
  • Oro Ferrari for heritage and luxury moments
Don't — Avoid
  • Rosso Corsa for text or large backgrounds
  • Rounded corners above 8px (pill only for tags)
  • More than two typeface weights per composition
  • White text on Rosso Corsa — use warm white
  • Busy gradients or decorative glows
  • Playful colors or saturated secondaries
  • Compressed spacing below 4px grid
