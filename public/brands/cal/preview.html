Guidelines

Do's & Don'ts

Do — Embrace
  • White (#ffffff) as the canvas background for all surfaces
  • Charcoal (#171717) for all primary text and headings
  • Night (#000000) for primary CTAs and brand moments
  • Sky (#006bff) for links and focus rings only
  • Pill shape (9999px) for all buttons and time selectors
  • 12px radius for cards with subtle Pearl borders
  • JetBrains Mono for all time displays and durations
  • Subtle, neutral shadows for elevation
Don't — Avoid
  • Dark backgrounds for primary content areas
  • Sky (#006bff) for button backgrounds
  • Hard, sharp corners on buttons or selectors
  • Heavy, colorful shadows or dark elevation
  • Dense packing of scheduling information
  • Iron (#737373) for primary body text
  • JetBrains Mono for non-time content
  • Decorative abstraction over functional clarity
