Guidelines

Do's & Don'ts

Do — Embrace
  • Warm Linen (#f9f8f6) as primary background
  • Bronze gradient for primary CTAs
  • Amber Glow (#f2b98b) for all links
  • Tangerine (#fb7232) for active states only
  • 8px radius for cards, 16px for panels
  • Generous 80px section gaps
  • Serif (Chronicle) for editorial warmth
Don't — Avoid
  • Pure white (#ffffff) backgrounds
  • Cool grays or blue-toned neutrals
  • Hard black (#000000) for text
  • Dense packing without breathing room
  • Monospace fonts for body content
  • Heavy drop shadows
  • Bright saturated colors outside bronze family
