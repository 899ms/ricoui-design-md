Guidelines

Do's & Don'ts

Do �?Embrace
  • Canvas White (#ffffff) as primary background
  • Midnight Navy (#050038) for all primary text
  • Sunshine Yellow (#FFD02F) for primary CTAs only
  • Sky Blue (#4ea8de) for links and info states
  • 12px radius for buttons, 16px for cards
  • 4px-based spacing with generous breathing room
  • Soft tint backgrounds for status feedback
Don't �?Avoid
  • Midnight Navy for large background areas
  • Yellow text on light backgrounds (low contrast)
  • Sharp corners on cards or buttons
  • More than two accent colors per section
  • Dense packing without breathing room
  • Decorative gradients on functional UI
  • Coral Pop for non-error contexts
