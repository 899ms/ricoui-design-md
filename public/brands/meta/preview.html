Guidelines

Do's & Don'ts

Do �?Embrace
  • White (#ffffff) as primary background for all surfaces
  • Dark Text (#1c1e21) for all primary body and heading text
  • Facebook Blue (#0668e1) for primary CTAs and links only
  • Off-White (#f0f2f5) for secondary surfaces and inputs
  • 6px radius for buttons, 8px for cards, 9999px for pills
  • 4px-based spacing with 8px elementGap for social density
  • Border-defined elevation over heavy box shadows
Don't �?Avoid
  • Facebook Blue for large background areas �?interactive only
  • Dark Text on dark backgrounds �?always check contrast
  • Hard sharp corners (0px) on cards or buttons
  • Overly saturated colors for body text
  • Dense packing without social-friendly breathing room
  • Font families outside Facebook Sans / system fallbacks
  • Monospace fonts for non-code content
