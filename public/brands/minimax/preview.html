Guidelines

Do's & Don'ts

Do �?Embrace
  • Snow White (#ffffff) as primary background
  • Ink (#1a1c23) for all primary text
  • Electric Blue (#4d6bfe) for CTAs and links
  • Violet (#8b5cf6) for AI-specific elements
  • 12px radius for buttons, 20px for chat bubbles
  • 4px-based spacing with generous breathing room
  • Subtle borders over heavy shadows
Don't �?Avoid
  • Dark backgrounds for primary content
  • Saturated colors for large surfaces
  • Hard sharp corners on interactive elements
  • Heavy, dark drop shadows
  • Dense packing without visual breathing room
  • Monospace fonts for non-code content
  • Coral for non-error contexts
