06 / Guidelines

Keep it recognizable.

Concrete implementation guardrails for preserving the visual character of Together AI.

Do / Embrace

  • Alternate dark infrastructure bands with clear content sections.
  • Use the orange–magenta–periwinkle rail as one gesture.
  • Pair display sans with uppercase mono labels.
  • Keep data cards crisp and grid-aligned.

Don't / Avoid

  • Do not split the spectrum into unrelated accent colors.
  • Do not use heavy rounded consumer-app cards.
  • Do not hide technical hierarchy behind decoration.
  • Do not dilute dark hero contrast with gray text.
