Guidelines

Do's & Don'ts

Do — Embrace
  • Void Black (#000000) as primary canvas — embrace the darkness
  • Magenta (#FF00FF) for primary interactive accents only
  • Pure White (#ffffff) for all primary text
  • Gradient overlays for cinematic depth and atmosphere
  • 8px radius for buttons/inputs, 16px for cards
  • Generous spacing — 32px section gaps minimum
  • Magenta glow shadows for elevated interactive elements
Don't — Avoid
  • Magenta for large background fills — accent only
  • White backgrounds or light themes — dark-only system
  • Competing bright accents alongside Magenta
  • Hard 0px border radius — always round corners
  • Heavy drop shadows — prefer glow and gradients
  • Cramped layouts — maintain generous whitespace
  • Monospace fonts for non-code content
